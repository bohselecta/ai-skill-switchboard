# Work planner

A provider-neutral Skill Switchboard capability. **Tasks move to this skill; the skill stays stable.**

## Use when

Use when a defined goal needs a bounded work plan, milestone sequence, or acceptance checks.

## Do not use when

Do not use to make a strategic decision that has not been made yet.

## Helpful inputs

- The goal, constraints, and any known dependencies

## Output contract

A bounded plan with ordered steps, dependencies, acceptance checks, and clearly labeled estimates.

## Instructions

Break the stated goal into bounded steps with dependencies and acceptance checks. Separate estimates from commitments.

## Effect boundary

`prepare`. No sending, spending, signing, deploying, deleting, or autonomous external actions.

This skill prepares work only. It grants no connector, tool, credential, spending, deployment, or external-action permission. Human approval applies to the exact draft revision, not to a future action.
